Common questions

Where are my codes saved?
Only on this device. QR Studio does not create an account and does not sync to a server.

How do I export a code?
Open a code, go to Export, then share a PNG for quick use or an SVG for print shops.

Why does the app warn me about contrast?
Low-contrast colors can fail after printing. Prefer dark modules on a light background, and leave quiet space around the code.

How do I delete saved codes?
Swipe a saved code to delete it, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored locally.
