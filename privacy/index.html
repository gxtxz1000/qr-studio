Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Summary

QR Studio stores the codes you save only on this device. We do not create an account, we do not collect personal information, and the app does not include advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

QR Studio may store the following locally so the app works offline:

  • QR codes you create and save, including the text you enter and style choices
  • Your preferred language and appearance settings

This information remains on your device. Uninstalling QR Studio deletes the codes and settings saved here.

What we do not collect

  • No account registration
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No sale of personal information

Sharing exports

When you export a PNG or SVG, QR Studio uses the system share sheet. The file is sent only to the app or service you choose. We do not operate a server that receives those files.

Children

QR Studio is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. Because the app does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly process children’s data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of QR Studio after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

For a privacy question, write to gxtxz1000@gmail.com.
